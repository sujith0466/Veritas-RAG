## 2. Architecture & Components Delivered

### 2.1 Backend Core & RBAC Layer (F4.4)
- **Central Permission Registry** ([registry.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/core/permissions/registry.py)):
  - 16 distinct permission categories covering Workspaces, Users, Invitations, Roles, Billing, Documents, Knowledge Base, Vector Storage, Chunks, Embeddings, Hybrid Retrieval, Reliability, Analytics, Executive Dashboard, AI Chat, and System Settings.
  - O(1) in-memory lookups via pre-computed role bitsets.
  - Explicit deny semantics for suspended users (`is_suspended=True`).
- **Role Hierarchy & Enums** ([rbac.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/core/permissions/rbac.py)):
  - Platform-level roles (`platform_admin`) and Workspace-level hierarchy (`owner > admin > member > viewer`, plus granular roles `engineer`, `analyst`).
- **FastAPI RBAC Dependencies** ([rbac.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/core/dependencies/rbac.py) & [auth.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/core/dependencies/auth.py)):
  - `require_permission(*permissions)` and `require_role(*roles)` for declarative route-level authorization.

### 2.2 Repositories & Data Access (F4.2 & F4.3)
- **WorkspaceMemberRepository** ([workspace_member.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/repositories/workspace_member.py)):
  - Keyset / cursor-based pagination and offset pagination for large enterprise organizations.
  - Multi-tenant tenant boundary checks and active member counts.
  - Pessimistic locking methods: `get_by_id_for_update` and `get_membership_for_update`.
  - Active owner counting (`count_active_owners`) for Last Owner Protection.

### 2.3 Service Layer
- **WorkspaceInvitationService — Acceptance Flow (F4.2)** ([invitation_service.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/services/workspace/invitation_service.py)):
  - Atomic verification and acceptance under pessimistic token locking.
  - Strict invitee email matching check against authenticated `UserContext`.
  - Idempotent handling and prevention of duplicate memberships.
  - Automatic audit logging and `WorkspaceInvitationAcceptedEvent` dispatching.
- **WorkspaceMembershipService (F4.3)** ([membership_service.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/services/workspace/membership_service.py)):
  - Role mutation with role hierarchy enforcement (e.g., ADMIN cannot promote to OWNER or modify OWNER).
  - Last Owner Protection invariant ensuring a workspace cannot be left without an active OWNER.
  - Member suspension, restoration, and soft removal lifecycle.
  - Bulk member management (up to 100 members per batch) with aggregate audit log.
  - Domain events: `WorkspaceMemberRoleUpdatedEvent`, `WorkspaceMemberSuspendedEvent`, `WorkspaceMemberRestoredEvent`, `WorkspaceMemberRemovedEvent`.

### 2.4 API Routes & Schemas
- **Workspace Invitations Routes** ([workspace_invitations.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/api/v1/routes/workspace_invitations.py)):
  - `POST /invitations/accept`: Cryptographic token acceptance.
  - `GET /invitations/verify`: Magic link preview & metadata verification.
- **Workspace Members Routes** ([workspace_members.py](file:///d:/RAGuard/backend/api/v1/routes/workspace_members.py)):
  - `GET /workspaces/{id}/members`: List members with search, role filter, status filter, and pagination.
  - `GET /workspaces/{id}/members/{member_id}`: Retrieve member profile.
  - `PATCH /workspaces/{id}/members/{member_id}/role`: Update member role.
  - `POST /workspaces/{id}/members/{member_id}/suspend`: Suspend member.
  - `POST /workspaces/{id}/members/{member_id}/restore`: Restore member.
  - `DELETE /workspaces/{id}/members/{member_id}`: Soft remove member.
  - `POST /workspaces/{id}/members/bulk`: Bulk manage members.
